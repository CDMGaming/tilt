# TILT Jam pack: Rock

The tropes each template is built from: what makes it sound like that kind of rock. All riffs, grooves,
progressions and melodies in `src/jam/packs/rock.ts` are our own, written from these tropes as scale
degrees and rhythms; none are taken from real songs. Edit this doc and the data together.

Shared across the pack:
- **Lineup:** Garage drums, Pluck's bass guitar (Finger / Pick / Muted Bass), Axe guitars (one or two
  rhythm parts plus a lead), and sometimes keys (Parlor piano, Wheelhouse organ, Anthem pads).
- **Part cards:** Drums: Energy, Feel, Room, Crunch. Bass: Drive, Follow, Octave. Guitar: Gain, Tone,
  Riff, Width. Keys / lead: Brightness, Space, Riff. Each card also has Level.
- **Live sections:** Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Solo, Breakdown. Intro and outro come from the song form.
- **Safety:** everything is scale-locked to the template's modes. Riff, groove and section changes
  land on the next bar. The limiter is always on.

## Power Ballad

*Lighters up: a tender verse that blows up into a huge, slow chorus.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 66-78 bpm, straight, with half-time weight. Very little swing.
- **Keys / modes:** E, D, G, A, C; major (Ionian), with Mixolydian allowed for a bVII lift.
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: I-V-vi-IV, I-iii-IV-V, vi-IV-I-V.
  - Chorus: IV-V-I-vi, I-V-vi-IV, vi-IV-I-V.
  - Bridge: ii-V-iii-vi, IV-V-vi-V.
  - Breakdown: piano alone on IV-I.
- **Form:** Intro (piano) → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Verse → Chorus ×2 → Bridge → Solo ×2 → Breakdown →
  Chorus ×2 (key change) → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - Verse: rim clicks and a closed hat in 8ths, no snare.
  - Chorus: a slow, heavy backbeat with a big room and crashes on the 1.
  - Fills: tom rolls down the kit.
- **Bass:** whole and half notes on the root in the verse. Chorus: roots in 8ths with a step up to the next chord.
- **Guitars:**
  - Clean: an arpeggio that picks through the chord (1-5-8-3) in the verse; it rests in the chorus.
  - Distorted: sustained power chords in the chorus; it rests in the verse.
  - Lead: long held notes, with octave leaps in the solo.
- **Keys:** a grand piano: held chords and broken-chord arpeggios. It carries the intro and breakdown alone.
- **Signature moves:** the truck-driver key change (up a whole step for the last chorus). The verse
  stays clean and sparse, the chorus goes big. The breakdown drops to piano and voice.
- **FX flavour:** big reverb, gentle sweeps, long risers (2 bars).

## Hair Metal

*Swagger: chunky palm-muted eighths, gang-shout choruses, a shred solo with squeals.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 118-138 bpm, straight eighths, a little push on the kick.
- **Keys / modes:** E, A, D; Mixolydian (for the bVII) and major.
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: I-bVII-IV-I, I-I-bVII-IV.
  - Chorus: IV-I-bVII-I, I-IV-V-IV.
  - Bridge: vi-IV-V-V.
  - Breakdown: I pedal.
- **Form:** Intro → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Verse → Chorus ×2 → Solo ×2 → Breakdown → Chorus ×2 → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - A big gated snare on 2 and 4, kick on 1 and the "and" of 2, open hats in the chorus.
  - Breakdown: a stop-start backbeat.
- **Bass:** pumping root 8ths that lock to the kick, with octave pops in the chorus.
- **Guitars:**
  - Rhythm: palm-muted pedal chugs on the root with power-chord stabs on the chord changes. Chorus:
    open, ringing power chords.
  - Lead: fast scale runs, pick squeals at phrase ends, and a repeated hook in the chorus.
- **Keys:** a wide 80s synth pad under the chorus (Anthem).
- **Signature moves:** the pick squeal, palm-mute pedal riffs, a stop-time breakdown, and the chorus
  played twice at the end.
- **FX flavour:** huge reverb on the snare, a 16th stutter, short risers (1 bar).

## Hardcore

*Fast and furious: d-beat drive, two-step bounce, then a half-time breakdown that hits like a truck.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 160-196 bpm. The breakdown is half time.
- **Keys / modes:** E, D, C# (low); minor (Aeolian) and Phrygian (the bII).
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: i-i-bVI-bVII, i-bVII-bVI-bVII.
  - Chorus: bVI-bVII-i-i, i-bVI-bIII-bVII.
  - Bridge: i-bII-i-bII (Phrygian).
  - Breakdown: i (one chord, all rhythm).
- **Form:** Intro → Verse ×2 → Chorus → Verse ×2 → Chorus → Bridge → Breakdown ×2 → Chorus → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - Verse: a d-beat-style drive (kick and snare chasing each other in 8ths).
  - Chorus: a fast two-step with ride-like hats.
  - Breakdown: half time, kick hits doubling the guitar chugs.
- **Bass:** follows the guitar note for note; in the breakdown it hits with the chugs.
- **Guitars:**
  - Two rhythm guitars: downpicked 8th-note power chords, and palm-muted chug patterns in the breakdown.
  - The second guitar plays a higher, dissonant counter-riff (octave and fifth) in the bridge.
- **Keys:** none.
- **Signature moves:** the breakdown (half time, open low chugs with gaps), and the switch from d-beat drive
  to the two-step bounce. (Not yet: a full-band stop on the last beat before the chorus. Sections loop as
  scenes, so that needs its own pre-chorus scene.)
- **FX flavour:** dry and tight; 8th-note stutter, tape stop as the end hit.

## Punk

*Three chords and the truth: fast downstrokes, oom-pah drums, a shout-along chorus.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 160-190 bpm, straight and driving.
- **Keys / modes:** A, E, D, G; major (and Mixolydian for a bVII).
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: I-IV-V-IV, I-V-vi-IV.
  - Chorus: IV-V-I-I, I-IV-I-V.
  - Bridge: vi-IV-I-V.
  - Breakdown: bass and drums on I.
- **Form:** Intro → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Bridge → Chorus ×2 → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - The "oom-pah" two-beat (kick on the beats, snare on the offbeat 8ths) in the verse.
  - A straight rock beat with crashes in the chorus.
  - Fills: snare rolls.
- **Bass:** relentless root 8ths, with a walk up into the chorus.
- **Guitars:**
  - Rhythm: downstroked 8th-note power chords, with an open strum on the chorus 1.
  - Lead: a simple octave melody (the "second vocal") in the chorus.
- **Keys:** a cheap combo organ (Garage Organ) doubling the chorus melody.
- **Signature moves:** the snare-roll fill into every chorus, and a breakdown with just bass and drums.
- **FX flavour:** dry rooms, a quick 8th stutter.

## Arena Anthem

*Stomping, fists in the air: big drums, chugging eighths, a chorus the whole stadium sings.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 118-138 bpm, straight, a stomp in the kick.
- **Keys / modes:** E, D, A, G; major and Mixolydian.
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: I-V-vi-IV, I-IV-I-V, vi-IV-I-V.
  - Chorus: IV-I-V-vi, I-V-IV-V, IV-V-vi-I.
  - Bridge: vi-IV-I-V, ii-IV-vi-V.
  - Solo: over the chorus changes.
- **Form:** Intro → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Bridge → Solo ×2 → Breakdown →
  Chorus ×2 (key change) → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - A stomp groove: kick on 1 and 3 with pickups, snare on 2 and 4, crashes on the chorus 1.
  - Tom-heavy intro and fills.
- **Bass:** root 8ths, a gallop or a walk up.
- **Guitars:**
  - Rhythm: palm-muted 8ths in the verse, open power-chord 8ths in the chorus, big strums.
  - Lead: the chorus hook (step-wise, singable), soaring held notes in the solo.
- **Keys:** a rock organ: held chords and stabs.
- **Signature moves:** the key change up a whole step for the last chorus, and a half-time bridge.
- **FX flavour:** a big hall, 2-bar risers into the chorus.

## Grunge

*Quiet verse, LOUD chorus: murky clean picking, then a wall of sludgy fuzz.*

- **Tempo / feel:** 84-108 bpm, loose, slightly behind the beat.
- **Keys / modes:** E, D, F# (low); minor, Dorian and Phrygian.
- **Progressions:**
  - Verse: i-bVII-i-bVII, i-iv-i-bVI.
  - Chorus: bVI-bVII-i-i, i-bVI-bIII-bVII.
  - Bridge: iv-v-bVI-bVII, and bII-i (Phrygian colour).
  - Breakdown: i, sparse.
- **Form:** Intro (clean guitar) → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Verse ×2 → Chorus ×2 → Solo ×2 → Bridge → Chorus ×2
  → Outro.
- **Drums:**
  - Verse: a loose, roomy backbeat with ghost notes.
  - Chorus: heavier, with floor-tom drive and crash-ride wash.
- **Bass:** gritty and prominent (high Drive home); it plays the riff while the verse guitar is quiet.
- **Guitars:**
  - Clean: a murky single-note figure in the verse; it rests in the chorus.
  - Sludge: fuzz power chords, held and slurred, in the chorus.
  - Lead: a noisy solo that restates the verse figure.
- **Keys:** none.
- **Signature moves:** the quiet / loud dynamic (two guitars that take turns), the half-time bridge, and the
  solo restating the verse melody.
- **FX flavour:** a medium room, tape stop and filter sweep for the drop into the chorus.
